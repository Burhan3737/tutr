## [S&D-1 s2] Demand and supply curves

Two lines on a price (vertical) against quantity (horizontal) graph: demand slopes down, supply slopes up, and they cross at the equilibrium.

- Demand: the quantity buyers want falls as price rises.
- Supply: the quantity sellers offer rises as price rises.
- Equilibrium: where the two curves cross.

Example: $Q_d = 100 - 2P$ and $Q_s = 20 + 2P$. Setting $Q_d = Q_s$ gives $100 - 2P = 20 + 2P$, so $P = 20$ and $Q = 60$.

## [S&D-1 s3] Price elasticity

Elasticity measures how strongly quantity responds to price:

$$E = \frac{\%\ \text{change in quantity}}{\%\ \text{change in price}}$$

- $|E| > 1$: elastic. $|E| < 1$: inelastic.
- Example: price rises 10%, quantity falls 20%, so $E = -2$ (elastic).
